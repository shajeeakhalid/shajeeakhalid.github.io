Executive Overview

The proposition is not another stand-alone credit line. It is an NCGCL-centered platform that combines ADB capital, technical assistance and climate standards with PFI origination and disciplined risk retention.

Digital data supplements—not replaces—credit judgment, KYC/AML, borrower verification and enforceable repayment arrangements.

Borrowing-base logic

Illustrative PKR million example: eligible receivables/inventory are haircut for dilution, concentration and operational leakage before guarantee eligibility.

Early-warning triggers

RedProceeds diversion, duplicate financing, sharp refund/dilution spike.
AmberSales decline, inventory aging, buyer concentration or missed data feed.
GreenStable turnover, verified orders, on-time settlements and clean repayment.

Modular ask from ADB

1 • Diagnostic TAMarket sizing, borrower economics, PFI constraints, pipeline and climate additionality.
2 • Product preparationEligibility, underwriting, guarantees, claims, legal documentation and operating manual.
3 • Funded risk capitalFirst-loss reserve or funded risk participation calibrated to modeled loss.
4 • Counter-guaranteeShare NCGCL’s covered risk to expand capital-efficient portfolio capacity.
5 • PFI / MIS supportData standards, digital reporting, safeguards, climate tagging and capacity building.
6 • Scale capitalFIL, concessional line or mobilized cofinancing after pilot performance is evidenced.

Proposed preparation pathway

Joint diagnostic0–60 days • choose market, size pipeline, map barriers, validate climate logic and select PFIs.
Product design61–120 days • risk model, eligibility, pricing, legal structure, safeguards, MIS and MRV.
Controlled pilot6–18 months • one platform, limited windows, predefined caps, stop-loss and learning agenda.
Evidence-led scalePost-pilot • recalibrate using vintage performance; add PFIs, sectors and mobilized capital.

Five meeting asks

Include demand, pipeline, PFI constraints, risk calibration, safeguards and MRV.
